Generative scenes

Scenes that never repeat

Each scene is a small set of rules fed by one random generator. Nothing is scripted and nothing loops, so no two runs play the same way.

Scenes

  • Pixel City

    A pixel-art night city seen from a rooftop. Traffic, a metro, a cat on the ledge, pigeons, weather, flickering windows and neon.

  • Half Court

    One-on-one under the floodlights, in streetwear inspired by the Night City logo. Real ball physics off the rim and glass, crossovers, blocks, steals, streetball rules and a cat that wanders by.

How it works

  • One seedA single random generator, seeded from the clock when the page opens, drives every choice.
  • Independent timersEach kind of event waits a random gap, so the scene has bursts and lulls instead of a beat.
  • Variation inside eventsSpeed, colour, size and duration come from continuous ranges. No two taxis are quite the same taxi.
  • Slow driftSmooth noise shifts traffic, wind and mood over minutes, on top of the individual events.
